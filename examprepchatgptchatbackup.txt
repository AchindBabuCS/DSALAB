Incorrect

3. The merge function
Here's the pseudocode:


text
i = low
j = mid + 1
k = low

WHILE i <= mid AND j <= high

    IF arr[i] <= arr[j]
        temp[k] = arr[i]
        i++
    ELSE
        temp[k] = arr[j]
        j++

    k++

END WHILE

WHILE i <= mid
    temp[k] = arr[i]
    i++
    k++

WHILE j <= high
    temp[k] = arr[j]
    j++
    k++

COPY temp[low...high] BACK TO arr
There are three important stages:

Compare elements from both halves and copy the smaller one.

Copy any remaining elements from the left or right half.

Copy the merged result back into the original array.
